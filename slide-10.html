How Rentakoto earns

Four diversified, compounding revenue streams — each growing with platform usage.

Platform Transaction Fee

A small fee charged per successful rental secured through Rentakoto's end-to-end flow — paid by the landlord, renter, or split. Low friction, high frequency.

~2–5% per completed transaction

Landlord Pro Subscription

Monthly plan unlocking Smart Pricing AI, priority listing placement, tenant analytics dashboard, and automated vacancy management tools.

₱499–₱1,499/mo per landlord portfolio

Renter Premium Access

Free tier for basic search. Premium unlocks AI matchmaker alerts, full safety reports, priority viewing slots, and document signing tools.

₱149–₱299/mo per active renter

Service Provider Marketplace

Movers, cleaning services, internet providers, and utilities pay to be featured and referred at the point of lease signing — the highest-intent moment in the rental journey.

Referral fee + listing placement revenue
